Why Hupy

Everything you need to actually speak

Most apps stop at vocabulary. Hupy takes you from passive review to real conversation.

Realtime voice tutor

Hupy Live pairs you with an AI tutor that speaks back in real time — it listens to your voice, catches your mistakes, and corrects you the moment you finish your sentence.

Planet curriculum

Learn on a course of planets. Each planet is a stage of lessons built on conversation, so you progress from “Hi” to holding a full conversation — one world at a time.

Smart flashcards

Words you miss become cards scheduled with spaced repetition — reviews at 1, 3 and 7 days, then eased up to 90. The phrases you slip on resurface before you forget them.

Listening stories

Every planet ships an audio story. Tune your ear to natural speech, then replay any phrase and read along as you build comprehension.

Streaks & badges

Daily streaks, XP, levels and badges turn practice into a habit you want to keep. Mastery on each planet is measured against real speaking metrics — not just taps.

Private by design

Your credentials are encrypted on-device, you stay in control of your data, and deleting your account erases your progress and saved cards — permanently.
