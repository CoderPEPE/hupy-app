Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 16, 2026

At Hupy, we believe privacy is not a feature — it is a promise. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you use the Hupy app, why we collect it, how it is used and shared, and the choices you have. It applies to the Hupy mobile app (the “Service”), and by using the Service you agree to the practices described below.

1. Information we collect

We practice data minimization: we collect only what is needed to run and improve the learning experience.

  • Account information. When you sign in with Google or register with an email, we receive your name, email address and profile picture, together with your chosen learning language.
  • Learning progress. Your planet progression, lesson history, mastery metrics, streaks, experience points, levels and badges.
  • Conversation content. Transcripts of your conversations with the AI tutor and any corrections you save, so you can review them later.
  • Flashcards. Cards you create and your review ratings, used to schedule spaced repetition.
  • Voice and audio. When you practice speaking, audio is processed to power your voice conversation with the tutor, including transcription and speech synthesis.
  • Usage and device information. Basic technical data such as app version, device model and operating system, used to keep the Service working and to diagnose issues.

2. How we use your information

  • To provide, personalize and improve the tutoring, lessons, flashcards, listening stories and gamification features.
  • To process speech and operate the realtime AI tutor — transcribing your voice and generating responses and corrections.
  • To generate text-to-speech audio for the phrases you choose to hear.
  • To keep your session secure and, where applicable, sync your progress across devices.
  • To respond to support requests and communicate important service updates.
  • To enforce our Terms of Service and protect the safety and integrity of the Service.

3. Microphone and device permissions

Hupy uses your device’s microphone so you can practice speaking. The microphone is active only during a practice session with the tutor, and access is requested through your device’s permission system. You can revoke microphone access at any time from your device settings. We never record, listen to or store audio outside the practice sessions you start yourself.

4. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for advertising or any unrelated purpose. We share data only where it is necessary to deliver the Service:

  • Google. When you sign in with Google, we use Google’s authentication to verify your identity. We receive only the profile details you consent to share.
  • AI providers. Conversation audio and text may be processed by third-party AI services (such as OpenAI) to generate tutor responses, corrections and speech synthesis. These providers process the data to perform the service and may retain it in line with their own policies for safety and abuse-prevention.
  • Hosting providers. Our servers and databases are operated by cloud infrastructure providers that store your data on our behalf.
  • Legal obligations. We may disclose information where required by law, or to protect the rights, safety and security of our users, the Service or the public.

5. International data transfers

Your information may be stored and processed in countries other than your own, including where our servers and the third-party providers described above are located. Where we transfer data internationally, we rely on appropriate safeguards, such as standard contractual clauses, to protect it.

6. Data retention and deletion

We retain your account and learning data for as long as your account is active. You can delete your account at any time from the Profile screen in the app (“Delete account”). Deleting your account erases your account and all associated progress, streaks and saved cards — it cannot be undone. Where required by law, limited records may be retained for legal or security purposes after deletion.

7. How we protect your information

  • All traffic to our servers is encrypted in transit using HTTPS.
  • Your login credentials are encrypted on your device and stored in your device’s secure storage (iOS Keychain / Android Keystore), bound to your device.
  • Passwords are hashed and never stored in plain text; access tokens are short-lived and rotated, and only a hash of refresh tokens is stored server-side.
  • Access to our backend is restricted to authorized personnel, and our code is developed and reviewed with security in mind.

No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. While we work hard to protect your data, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Children’s privacy

The Service is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

9. Your rights and choices

  • Review and update your display name at any time in the app.
  • Choose your tutor’s voice and your learning language.
  • Delete your account and all associated data directly from the app.
  • Revoke microphone access at any time through your device settings.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, export or erase your personal data — contact us and we will help you exercise them.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the “Last updated” date at the top of this page and notify you of significant changes in the app. Continued use of the Service after changes take effect means you accept the updated policy.

11. Contact us

Questions about this policy or about your data? Write to us at support@hupy.app and we will get back to you promptly.
